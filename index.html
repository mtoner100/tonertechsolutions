Newport Apartment Hunt

A tracker for a 4-bedroom, 12-month rental search across Newport and Middletown, Rhode Island. Listings are scored on parking, storage, lease length and move-in date, with a kanban board, a map, and a tour calendar. Agents on my Mac keep it fed.
